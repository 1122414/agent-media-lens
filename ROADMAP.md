# Roadmap

v0.1.0 covers public Douyin video, local processing, SQLite, MCP, CLI and the first Codex plugin. The [release report](docs/releases/v0.1.0.md) documents the measured scope and platform limits.

The local implementation after v0.1.0 adds page-indexed Douyin albums and Bilibili guest video with part selection. Both have live acceptance cases. Next, investigate native public video acquisition, a live positive case for the experimental Xiaohongshu guest adapter, a YouTube adapter, local MP4 and audio inputs, other agent clients, media knowledge bases and cross-video retrieval. Each adapter needs permission boundaries, measured contracts and platform-specific test coverage.
